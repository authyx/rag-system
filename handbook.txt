1.  REMOTE WORK POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------
At our company, we embrace flexible work arrangements. All full-time
employees are eligible to work remotely up to 4 days per week,
subject to manager approval and role suitability.

Equipment & Connectivity:
- The company provides a laptop, monitor, and headset.
- Employees must have a reliable high-speed internet connection
  (minimum 50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload).
- You are responsible for maintaining a secure, private workspace
  free from distractions during working hours.

Communication & Availability:
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (local time).
  All team members should be online and reachable via Slack during
  this window.
- Respond to emails and Slack messages within 2 business hours.
- Video cameras should be on during scheduled team meetings to
  foster connection.

Work Hours & Breaks:
- You may set your own start/end times, but you must log at least
  8 hours per day (including a 1-hour lunch break).
- Take short breaks every 90 minutes to avoid burnout; we
  encourage stepping away from your screen.

Reimbursement:
- A monthly stipend of $50 is provided to offset home internet and
  electricity costs. Submit receipts via the expense portal.

2.  VACATION POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------
We believe in work-life balance and encourage employees to take
time off to recharge.

Accrual:
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year,
  prorated for the first year.
- Accrual occurs monthly (1.25 days per month) and carries over
  up to a maximum of 30 days.

Request & Approval:
- Vacation requests must be submitted via the HR system at least
  2 weeks in advance for 3+ consecutive days.
- For single days, at least 48 hours' notice is required.
- Managers will approve or deny requests within 3 business days,
  considering team coverage and project deadlines.

Holidays & Sick Leave:
- In addition to vacation, the company observes 10 paid public
  holidays (see appendix for list).
- Sick leave is separate: 10 paid sick days per year, no advance
  notice required (just inform your manager as soon as possible).

Payout & Unused Time:
- Unused vacation days are paid out upon termination of employment.
- You may donate unused vacation days to a colleague in need
  (subject to HR approval).

3.  ADDITIONAL REMINDERS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Confidentiality: All company data and client information must
  be handled with care. Use VPN when accessing internal systems
  from public networks.
- Performance Reviews: Quarterly check-ins with your manager;
  annual performance reviews in December.
- Code of Conduct: Respect, inclusivity, and professionalism are
  mandatory. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
